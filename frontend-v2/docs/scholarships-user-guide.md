# Scholarships user guide

## Overview
ChainVerse scholarships let you apply for funded learning support. As a student, you build an eligibility
profile, grant limited evidence permission, and upload any required supporting documents. A reviewer then
evaluates your application against the published scholarship rules, and funded programs release milestone
payments in XLM when you complete each stage.

## Applying for a scholarship
1. Open the **Scholarships** section and select an eligible program.
2. Confirm your enrollment status, course track, and income band. Only the minimum data needed to evaluate
   the rules is collected.
3. Grant evidence permission for the specific checks the scholarship requires. Granting permission is
   affirmative — it is never inferred from visiting the page.
4. Upload supporting documents through a private, short-lived upload session. Documents are encrypted at rest
   and are never shown as public URLs.
5. Submit. You will see a deterministic eligibility decision with the matched rules and any missing evidence.

## Privacy
- Only the data required to evaluate the rules is requested and stored.
- Income band, attestation prompts, and geography are treated as sensitive evidence and are disclosed to
  sponsors only with your permission.
- Consent is versioned. If the terms, privacy notice, or sponsor disclosure changes, you will be asked for
  fresh consent before your application can continue.
- You can revoke evidence permission at any time; revoking prevents new evaluations while preserving the
  prior audit record.
- Documents are scanned, access is authenticated and logged, and records follow the privacy retention policy.

## Troubleshooting
- **No decision shown after submitting** — refresh and retry. Decisions are deterministic, so the same profile
  always produces the same result.
- **Asked to re-confirm consent** — the policy version changed; review the new terms and grant fresh consent.
- **Upload rejected** — check the file type and size limits shown on the upload control before requesting a
  session.
- **Still stuck?** Contact student support with your application reference only — never share documents or
  private evidence in chat.
